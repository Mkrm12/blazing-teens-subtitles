Season 1 Story Arcs & 40-Episode Guide (2006)

Originally broadcast in 2006 and produced by Alpha Group / Auldey, Blazing Teens Season 1 spans 40 episodes following transfer student Li Fei (Leo) as he joins the Jianghai No. 1 Middle School yo-yo team under Coach Gao Dashu (Coach Tony) and leads them toward the Autumn Yo-Yo Championship:

Episodes 01–10: Return from the U.S. & Team Formation

Leo transfers to Jianghai High after studying in the United States, clashes with vice-captain Mark (Mai Jun) and team captain Mini (Mi Li), uncovers his martial-arts-inspired Eastern Intuition play style, and earns his place on the Jianghai Yo-Yo Team with Fire Warrior.

Episodes 11–20: The Chengnan Rivalry & Dark Yo-Yo Duels

Rival captain Jacky (Chen Hao) of Chengnan Team and his masked cousin Marco (Ma Kai) challenge Jianghai with aggressive tricks and high-risk wagers. Leo refines his string control with Grandpa Li's five-stage diabolo philosophy (Learn, Master, Refine, Perfect, Transcend).

Episodes 21–30: Falcon Base Camp & Signature Moves

Jianghai and Chengnan enter rigorous boot-camp training at Falcon Base under Coach Tony and Coach Sarah. Players unlock customized Auldey signature yo-yos and master advanced 1A/2A/4A sleeper and off-string maneuvers ahead of the regional qualifiers.

Episodes 31–40: Saint Qiao Team & The Autumn Finals

Former California champion Michael (Maike) arrives with Coach William's powerhouse Saint Qiao Team, pitting Western Speed (Invincible Eagle, Wild Wolf Impact) against Jianghai's Eastern Intuition (Holy Light, Thousand Dragons Rising) in the Championship Finals.

About the 4-Pillar Extraction Pipeline

Because surviving recordings of the 2006 series contain burned-in Chinese text and loud action audio that cause standalone Whisper models to hallucinate, the localization pipeline fuses four independent signals per episode: OpenAI Whisper (audio transcription + stutter deduplication), PyAnnote (speaker diarization), EasyOCR + PaddleOCR (dual-box frame master clock for burned-in dialogue and title cards), and Microsoft Florence-2 (visual scene context), followed by DeepSeek + Gemini translation and 4 manual quality-control passes in player.html.
