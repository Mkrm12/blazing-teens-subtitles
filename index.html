About the 4-Pillar Extraction Pipeline

Because surviving episodes of the 2006 series contain burned-in Chinese text and noisy action audio that cause standalone Whisper models to hallucinate, the repository combines Whisper (audio + stutter deduplication), PyAnnote (speaker diarization), EasyOCR + PaddleOCR (dual-box frame master clock), and Florence-2 (scene context), followed by DeepSeek + Gemini localization and 4 manual QC passes standardizing Auldey yo-yo models and trick terminology.

FAQ

Which series is this for?
The original 2006 Chinese live-action Blazing Teens (火力少年王, Season 1, 40 episodes) following Li Fei / Leo and the Jianghai yo-yo team — not the later animated spin-offs.
Are all 40 episodes included?
Yes. Episodes 1–40 are each a separate .srt file, and all 40 are also bundled in one .zip.
Are these raw machine translations?
No. The pipeline produced the first draft; every episode then went through 4 manual QC passes for timing, accuracy, character names, and yo-yo terminology.
Which video do they sync to?
The 40-episode Mandarin broadcast — the same episodes uploaded on the official 火力少年王 Blazing Teens YouTube channel (@blazingteens2484).
Is this the same pack that's on other subtitle sites?
Yes. The same Season 1 pack (one .zip, 307 KB) was uploaded by the author to subtitle databases such as Podnapisi on 22 August 2026. This site and the GitHub repository are the primary, maintained source.
