About the 4-Pillar Extraction Pipeline

Because surviving episodes of the 2006 series contain burned-in Chinese text and noisy action audio that cause standalone Whisper models to hallucinate, the repository combines Whisper (audio + stutter deduplication), PyAnnote (speaker diarization), EasyOCR + PaddleOCR (dual-box frame master clock), and Florence-2 (scene context), followed by DeepSeek + Gemini localization and 4 manual QC passes standardizing Auldey yo-yo models and trick terminology.
